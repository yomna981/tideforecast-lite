Dahab Marine Spots • Tide & Wind Guidance

Local reef entry clearance, sandbar exposures, and tidal drift navigation advice
Dahab Lagoon • Kitesurf & Windsurf
At High Tide (>0.75m), the inner lagoon offers deep butter-flat water ideal for all levels. At Low Tide (<0.35m), outer sandbars emerge, creating speed strips while shallow reef flats require caution when launching.
Lighthouse & Eel Garden Reefs
Fringing coral reef entry. During Low Tide, cross only via marked sandy pathways to protect fragile corals. During High Tide, entry over the reef crest has comfortable clearance for freedivers and snorkelers.
Blue Hole & The Canyon
Open deep-water drop-offs. Tides affect surface chop and mild north-south littoral drift along the outer wall. Tidal transitions (slack water) offer calmest entry conditions at the Bells chimney.
